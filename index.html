SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY

Discovery is not analysis.

Satellite catalogue records identify available scenes. They do not establish environmental change. Processing, cloud and shadow screening, co-registration, uncertainty estimation and independent validation are required before publishing change metrics.

Read pipeline methodology
THERMAL DATA LIMITATION

Hotspot detections are not crater temperature.

FIRMS VIIRS and MODIS records are point detections at coarse spatial resolution. They must not be interpreted as calibrated crater-temperature measurements.
