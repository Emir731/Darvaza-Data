SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY

Finding an image is not the same as proving a change.

Satellite catalogue records identify available scenes. They do not establish environmental change. Processing, cloud and shadow screening, co-registration, uncertainty estimation and independent validation are required before publishing change metrics.

Read pipeline methodology
THERMAL DATA INTERPRETATION

Land-surface temperature is not flame temperature.

Landsat ST_B10 estimates the temperature of the ground surface. Its thermal detail is about 100 m here, even when the file is displayed on a finer grid. It does not directly measure the flame, subsurface gas or crater-core temperature. FIRMS VIIRS/MODIS detections are coarse-resolution hotspot points, not calibrated crater-temperature measurements.
